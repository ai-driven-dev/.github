## 🚀 The Framework is now open source

For three years we built one method: working with AI without letting go of the wheel. It's now free and open, packaged as a **marketplace of plugins** you add to your AI assistant. It covers the whole flow, from **brainstorm** to **plan, implement, review and ship**, with you in control at each step.

🤖 Built for **Claude Code**. Also Cursor, Copilot, Codex and OpenCode.

[![Open the Framework](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open_the_Framework-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ai-driven-dev/aidd-framework)
